Current support channel

Full Tilt currently provides support through the official Full Tilt community/Discord where access to the service was provided. Contact the Full Tilt operator or designated support staff there using the same Discord account you use to sign in to Full Tilt.

Purchase support

When paid cosmetics launch, support requests involving a charge should include your Discord username, the cosmetic purchased, approximate purchase date, and any receipt or payment reference. Never send a full payment-card number or card security code.

Account or gameplay support

For login, missing cosmetics, private table, tournament, or lifetime-stat problems, include the game/table code if applicable and a brief description of what happened.

Security reports

If you believe you found a security issue, do not exploit it against other users. Contact the Full Tilt operator privately through the official community so the issue can be investigated.
