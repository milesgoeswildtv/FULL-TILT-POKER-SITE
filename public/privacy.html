Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026. This page explains the information Full Tilt uses to operate accounts, games, and future cosmetic purchases.

Information we receive from Discord

Full Tilt uses Discord OAuth for account login. Depending on the Discord information available to the application, Full Tilt may receive and store your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar reference. We do not need your Discord password and do not receive it.

Game and account data

We store information needed to run the service, including your Full Tilt account record, equipped and owned cosmetics, private game-session information, play-chip game statistics, tournament results, and related service records.

Authentication data

Full Tilt uses signed session cookies and server-side account records to keep you logged in and protect poker APIs from unauthenticated access. Session data is used for security and account continuity.

Payment data

When paid cosmetic checkout is enabled, payments will be handled by a third-party payment processor. Full Tilt may receive transaction IDs, payment status, product information, amounts, and timestamps needed to fulfill and support an order. Full Tilt does not intend to store full card numbers or card security codes.

How information is used

  • Authenticate your account and display your player identity
  • Run private tables and multi-table tournaments
  • Maintain lifetime statistics and account-owned cosmetics
  • Fulfill and support cosmetic purchases
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, cheating, and unauthorized access
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain the service

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate Full Tilt, such as Discord for authentication, Cloudflare for hosting and infrastructure, and a payment processor when purchases are enabled. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or its legal rights.

Public and private game information

Other players may see your displayed Full Tilt/Discord name, avatar-derived identity, equipped cosmetics, table-visible stack, game actions, and game results. Private authentication credentials, session tokens, hidden cards, and internal account identifiers are not intended to be exposed to other players.

Retention

Account and game records may be retained while needed to provide the service, preserve player history, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and meet operational or legal obligations. Retention practices may evolve as the service develops.

Your choices

You can log out at any time. For questions about account data or a request concerning your information, use the Contact & Support page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Full Tilt adds features or service providers. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
