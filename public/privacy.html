Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026. This page explains the information Full Tilt uses to operate accounts, games, Telegram Mini App access, and cosmetic purchases.

Information we receive from identity providers

On the website, Full Tilt uses Discord OAuth for account login. Depending on the Discord information available to the application, Full Tilt may receive and store your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar reference. We do not need your Discord password and do not receive it.

When Full Tilt is opened as a Telegram Mini App, Telegram supplies signed launch data that can include your Telegram user ID, username, first and last name, profile-photo URL, launch parameter, and technical launch context. Full Tilt sends the raw signed launch data to its own server for validation before trusting the included user information. The Telegram bot token used to validate this data is kept server-side and is not sent to the browser.

Game and account data

We store information needed to run the service, including your Full Tilt account record, identity-provider reference, equipped and owned cosmetics, private game-session information, play-chip game statistics, tournament results, and related service records.

Authentication data

Full Tilt uses signed session cookies and server-side account records to keep you logged in and protect poker APIs from unauthenticated access. Telegram Mini App launch data is validated using Telegram's signed-data procedure before a Full Tilt session is issued. Session data is used for security and account continuity.

Payment data

When paid cosmetic checkout is enabled on the website, payments are handled by a third-party payment processor. Full Tilt may receive transaction IDs, payment status, product information, amounts, and timestamps needed to fulfill and support an order. Full Tilt does not intend to store full card numbers or card security codes. Digital purchases are not currently offered inside the Telegram Mini App.

How information is used

  • Authenticate your account and display your player identity
  • Run private tables and multi-table tournaments across supported Full Tilt clients
  • Maintain lifetime statistics and account-owned cosmetics
  • Fulfill and support cosmetic purchases where offered
  • Validate Telegram Mini App launches and supported game invite parameters
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, cheating, and unauthorized access
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain the service

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate Full Tilt, such as Discord or Telegram for authentication and platform functionality, Cloudflare for hosting and infrastructure, and a payment processor when purchases are enabled. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or its legal rights.

Public and private game information

Other players may see your displayed Full Tilt name, provider-supplied avatar where available, equipped cosmetics, table-visible stack, game actions, and game results. Private authentication credentials, signed launch data, session tokens, hidden cards, and internal account identifiers are not intended to be exposed to other players.

Retention

Account and game records may be retained while needed to provide the service, preserve player history, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and meet operational or legal obligations. Retention practices may evolve as the service develops.

Your choices

You can log out at any time. For questions about account data or a request concerning your information, use the Contact & Support page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Full Tilt adds features or service providers. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
