Cosmetic Purchases

Effective September 11, 2026. This page explains exactly what Full Tilt sells and what those purchases do.

Paid checkout is not live yet. Full Tilt is currently preparing its cosmetic shop. No purchase will be charged until live checkout is explicitly enabled.

What we sell

Full Tilt sells optional digital cosmetic customization only. Examples include:

  • Card-back designs
  • Chip designs
  • Player plaques
  • Avatar frames
  • Coordinated visual booster packs

What we do not sell

Full Tilt does not sell play chips, cashable currency, real-money poker entry, monetary prizes, betting credit, cash-out rights, or anything redeemable for money or material value.

No gameplay advantage

Cosmetics do not change cards, randomization, poker odds, betting rules, tournament eligibility, stack size, blinds, game outcomes, or any competitive advantage.

Account delivery

Purchased cosmetics are delivered to the Discord-linked Full Tilt account that completed checkout. Once fulfilled, an owned cosmetic can be equipped from the player's profile and follows that account across supported devices and tables.

License and transferability

A cosmetic purchase grants a personal, non-transferable right to use that digital item inside Full Tilt while the item and service remain available. Cosmetics cannot be sold, traded, gifted, transferred between accounts, exchanged for play chips, or redeemed for cash.

Payment processing

When checkout launches, payments will be processed by a third-party payment processor. Full Tilt will receive payment status and transaction identifiers needed to fulfill the purchase, but will not store full payment-card numbers or card security codes.

Pricing

The checkout screen will show the exact item and price before payment. The server, not the browser, determines the valid product and price associated with each booster.

Problems with an order

If a purchase is charged but the cosmetic is not delivered, or if you believe you were charged twice, contact support and include the Discord account used for the purchase plus any receipt or payment reference available to you.
