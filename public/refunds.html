Refund Policy

Effective September 11, 2026. This policy applies only to optional paid cosmetic digital items sold by Full Tilt Poker.

Paid checkout is not live yet. This policy is published in advance so customers can review it before purchases become available.

Digital cosmetic items

Because cosmetics are digital items delivered directly to a Full Tilt account, completed and successfully delivered purchases are generally final once access to the item has been granted, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

When we will review a refund

We will review requests involving:

  • A duplicate charge for the same intended purchase
  • A completed charge where the purchased cosmetic was not delivered
  • A technical fulfillment error that cannot reasonably be corrected
  • An unauthorized transaction reported by the account holder
  • Any circumstance where applicable law requires a refund

Changed your mind

For a correctly delivered digital cosmetic that has already been made available on the account, change-of-mind refunds may not be available except where required by law.

How to request help

Use the Contact & Support page. Include the Discord account used for Full Tilt, the cosmetic purchased, approximate purchase date, and any receipt or payment reference you have. Do not send full card numbers or card security codes.

Processing

Approved refunds will be sent back through the original payment method when possible. Timing after approval depends on the payment processor and financial institution.

Chargebacks

Please contact support first when possible so we can investigate delivery or duplicate-charge problems. Nothing in this policy limits rights provided by your bank, card network, payment provider, or applicable law.
